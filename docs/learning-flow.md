# Learning flow

The existing play route now owns a continuous sequence: a brief recall when due, the lesson's exercises, reading and transfer, then completion with skill states. No additional navigation screen is needed to access teaching, explanations, or personalized review.

## Teaching and assessment

Existing letter exercise content and ordering are preserved. A guide is embedded above the task; most lessons introduce their focus with up to three examples. Review checkpoints keep the examples out of the guide. Mistakes include an explanation, including the written composition of sound patterns and the article/noun relationship where supported. Matching mistakes retain their explanation rather than hiding it with a short tile animation.

Three formerly empty Unit 2 lesson slots now teach articles and nouns, joining names with ⲛⲉⲙ, and a mixed reading checkpoint. They offer word/morpheme building, missing letters, typed meanings and listening when a recording is available. Mark and Markos, and the person and the man, are accepted where the teaching supports both. Typed English accepts case, spacing and terminal punctuation variation.

Reading tasks let the learner inspect each taught phrase inline. Existing dictionary lookup supplies attested headwords, forms and supported article analyses. Dictionary links are provided only for actual returned entries. Early reading material consists of practice phrases and sound patterns, not invented connected stories or automatically generated grammar.

## Review and progress

`coptic-learning:v1` records first attempts, errors, due times and successful recall days. Missed items return after ten minutes; successful independent recalls receive a longer delay. Up to three due learned items appear at the beginning of a later lesson, including material from earlier units.

Mistakes also create or update real cards in the existing vocabulary review queue, preserving their spelling, meaning, explanation and recording. Immediate corrective retries cannot advance the review interval or inflate independent mastery. Hints likewise do not count as first-try recall.

The unit pathway and completion summary distinguish New, Needs practice, Building and Secure. Secure requires at least three successful independent recall days and at least 85% first-attempt accuracy. A newly wrong answer returns a previously secure item to Needs practice. Unit completion continues to use the existing keys and step IDs, so saved completion and vocabulary data remain intact. Clearing course progress also clears the new skill log, leaving the separate vocabulary deck in place.

Unreadable saved data is preserved and produces an error; it is not silently replaced. New stages reset their own scroll position. Choice panels remain independently scrollable on small screens, keeping the action footer usable.

## Recordings and content scope

Replay uses the bundled recordings; slow replay uses a 0.75 rate and normal replay resets to 1. The iOS player is prepared for rate control and returns its actual speed to native smoke checks. Neither mode substitutes synthetic speech. The missing “se Maria” clip is handled as a written exercise with replay disabled.

This is a shared learning upgrade and three new activities, not a complete 47-unit curriculum. Forty-five units still need authored and reviewed material. Phrase teaching reuses the existing course and bundled Bohairic dictionary: ⲣⲱⲙⲓ (Crum 294b), ⲙⲱⲟⲩ, ⲣⲁⲛ (Crum 297b), and the article forms under ⲡ-. Names and ⲛⲉⲙ phrases come from the existing name lessons.
